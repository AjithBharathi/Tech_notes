1️⃣ GCC – Global Capability Center
(Modern popular term)
A captive center of a multinational company in another country.
Example:
India tech center of Visa
India tech center of JPMorgan Chase
2️⃣ GIC – Global In-house Center
(Old name for GCC)
Before 2015–2018, companies used to call it GIC.
Same meaning as GCC.
GIC = Captive center owned by parent company.
3️⃣ Captive Center
Very important term in interviews.
👉 A delivery center fully owned by the parent company.
Not a third-party vendor.
Example:
India office of Goldman Sachs
It is their captive center.
4️⃣ Offshore Development Center (ODC)
Common in IT services.
👉 A dedicated offshore team created for one client.
Usually seen in:
Tata Consultancy Services
Infosys
Difference:
ODC = Service company serving a client
GCC = Company’s own center
5️⃣ Shared Services Center (SSC)
Focuses on support functions:
HR
Finance
Payroll
Procurement
More operations-oriented than product engineering.
6️⃣ CoE – Center of Excellence
A specialized team inside a company focused on:
AI
Cloud
DevOps
Security
Not necessarily a separate country center — more of a functional unit.
7️⃣ Global Delivery Center (GDC)
Used mostly by service companies.
Large multi-client delivery hubs.

👉 Company builds and sells its own software/product.
Revenue comes from selling product.
Examples:
Microsoft
Adobe
Salesforce
Focus:
Innovation
Product ownership
Engineering-heavy culture

👉 Works for multiple clients.
Revenue comes from client billing.
Examples:
Tata Consultancy Services
Infosys
Accenture
Focus:
Client projects
Outsourcing model
Billing-based revenue

👉 Early-stage product company.
Examples:
Razorpay
Swiggy
Zoho
High growth, high risk.

Sometimes companies are classified by industry.

Goldman Sachs

🏭 6️⃣ Enterprise / Industrial Tech Company
